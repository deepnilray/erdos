# PRINCIPIA Wachs polytope — independent audit, 8 November 2026

## Claim under audit
For positive m, the convex hull of the even Wachs permutations is affinely equivalent to the product of the standard m-permutohedron and an m-cube. This implies the conjectured simplicity.

## Rigorous mathematical check
The Wachs condition forces each adjacent value-pair to occupy adjacent positions. Such disjoint position-pairs tile [2m], hence are exactly (1,2),(3,4),...,(2m-1,2m). Their ordering gives pi in S_m; their independent orientations give epsilon in {±1}^m. The affine change of coordinates a_i=(x_(2i-1)+x_(2i)+1)/4, b_i=x_(2i)-x_(2i-1) sends each Wachs vector to (pi,epsilon), with inverse x_(2i-1)=2a_i-1/2-b_i/2, x_(2i)=2a_i-1/2+b_i/2. Taking convex hulls yields Perm_m × [-1,1]^m because conv(A×B)=conv(A)×conv(B). The standard permutohedron is simple and the cube is simple, so their product is simple.
