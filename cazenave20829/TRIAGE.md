# Candidate triage — 10 November 2026

Difficulty is a subjective selection score relative to RH = 100. The rows record the last status found before selection. The two spectral conjectures and Achour's two conjectures were read in the original documents. The Erdős rows were checked against the original problem statements, the [dated community status data](https://github.com/teorth/erdosproblems/blob/cda7dd1dbe089794eb1e9f4171edfc00ea0e5a90/data/problems.yaml), and the [9 November Formal Conjectures snapshot](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/tree/2f91d251c07fbbaf9435166d47c11e884a08a6cf/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems). For catalog pages with restricted direct fetches, archived full statements and primary sources were used. Follow-up searches were by mathematical object, including equivalent formulations and recent work.
