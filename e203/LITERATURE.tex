\textbf{Problem.} Is there $m$ with $(m,6)=1$ such that $2^k3^\ell m+1$ is composite for all $k,\ell$ (site form $k,\ell\ge0$; the repository works with $k,\ell\ge1$, which is the weaker YES statement and the stronger NO statement, so every repository exclusion of YES and every repository NO-witness transfers to the site form)?

\textbf{Status.} Open. Nothing in this document proves either answer. Labels used throughout: PROVED, VERIFIED (computation with a control that can fail), CONJECTURED, and for citations P (page opened this session or recorded as opened in an earlier ledger), S (second-hand), U (not opened).

\section{Methods that have not been used on \#203}

``Not used'' means absent from the repository's record and absent from the sources above. For items 2 and 6 the supporting search was a single query each, so absence there is weak evidence.

\begin{longtable}{P{0.6cm}P{3.6cm}P{7.7cm}P{3.1cm}}
\toprule
\# & Method & What it would do & Cost and caveat\\\midrule
\endhead
1 & Function-field analogue: $P^kQ^\ell m+1$ irreducible in $\mathbb F_q[T]$ with $P,Q$ coprime primes & Weil-type estimates and exact Chebotarev make both directions testable. Silverman's theorem (math/0401356, abstract level) says $\deg\gcd(a^n-1,b^n-1)\ge cn$ infinitely often there, so the analogue of $c(e)$ can be large, and a covering may exist in that setting. A covering found there would show which mechanism a covering over $\mathbb Z$ needs; a proof of non-existence there would show which step of the tail argument survives. & Moderate. CONJECTURED idea; the function-field covering paper above is the entry point.\\
2 & Maynard--Tao weights on the exponent box, in the variable $m$ & For a fixed admissible set of cells the forms $A_im+1$ are linear in $m$, so multidimensional sieve weights apply. The conclusion holds on a set of $m$ of density about $(\log x)^{-r}$, which does not reach a positive-density statement. & Low expected gain; listed so the question is closed.\\
3 & Two-dimensional Crittenden--Vanden Eynden principle & A $k$-coset periodic cover of a lattice line is decided by its first $2^k$ points. A planar analogue for cyclic-quotient cosets would turn a finite box into a statement about all of $\mathbb Z^2$ and convert the search data into a rigorous bound on $k$. & Unknown; needs a proof.\\
4 & Corvaja--Zannier / Hernandez--Luca for lattice alignments & Controls primes whose lattices share two independent vectors, which is the alignment quantity $c^<$ in \texttt{DISTORTION.md}. & Ineffective; finitely many unlisted exceptions.\\
5 & Klein--Koukoulopoulos--Lemieux multiplicity theorem on a lattice line & The direction lemma (Y7) gives a 1-D covering with moduli $e_p$ in the unit-direction case. Whether the multiplicity theorem was then applied is not recorded. & Cheap to check in \texttt{C\_allE.md}.\\
6 & Reformulation: $m$ is the $\{2,3\}$-free part of $p-1$ & NO is the statement that this map from primes with $6\mid p-1$ is onto the integers coprime to $6$. Results on smooth parts of shifted primes may apply. & One literature search not yet made.\\
7 & Exact LP/ILP duals at fixed torus size & Would sharpen Y1 for individual $N$, but Y1 already excludes every $N$ with all $e_p\le4\cdot10^5$. & Moot.\\
\bottomrule
