## (a) Verdict: what is new compared with the existing ledger

1. **The JNT 2024 function-field paper is now identified.** ScienceDirect PII S0022314X24001707 is DOI 10.1016/j.jnt.2024.07.002 (the doi.org redirect was checked). It is H. Li, B. Wang, C. Wang, S. Yi, "On Erdős covering systems in global function fields", J. Number Theory 266 (2025) 269–280, arXiv:2402.03810. I read the PDF. It covers the *ring* O_S by residue classes modulo ideals, which is the analogue of the 1-D minimum-modulus problem. It is **not** the analogue of the exponent-lattice covering in `ff/`, where the indices e_π are rational integers. So it does not apply to `ff/` directly. One technical point does carry over: the authors need only second moments once every modulus is large (row N16).
2. **The 1-D multiplicity theory exists, and the existing ledger does not cite it.** This is Filaseta–Ford–Konyagin–Pomerance–Yu, JAMS 2007 (row N9). Its Lemma 3.2 is the 1-D prototype of the repo's missing tail bound, with the same s² dependence on multiplicity. Its Theorem 5 builds exact coverings with all moduli > N once multiplicity up to exp(√(log N log log N)) is allowed. So in 1-D a multiplicity bound is *provably necessary*: the c(e) obstacle in HUB §2 is real, not an artefact of the method.
3. **The only covering paper with two exponents of a fixed base is Sun–Le (Acta Arith. 2001), building on Crocker 1971.** Its mechanism uses 2^a + 2^b = 2^b(2^{a−b}+1) and the Fermat-number chain on v₂(a−b) (row N23). It has no analogue for 2^k3^l·m+1, where each prime kills a coset. I found no paper or project on k·2^m·3^n+1, on "Sierpiński numbers in two bases", or on 3-smooth multipliers. The searches covered arXiv (API, titles and abstracts), WebSearch, OEIS and CRUS. The one exception is Guthmann's primality test for N = k·2^m·3^n+1 (BIT 1992, row N30), which is a computational tool, not a covering.
4. **New 2025–2026 covering papers, none of which mentions #203.** They are: Virbalas (Brier numbers in every base, Oct 2026); Filaseta–Kalogirou–Klein (Oct 2026); Harrington–Klein–Lowrance–Trifonov (third-order Bonferroni bound, strong infinite coverings, May 2026); Filaseta–Kalogirou (Trans. AMS 2026); Wang (Crittenden–Vanden Eynden for F_q[x]); the 2-D lcm/ILP construction papers; and a Lean-checked odd-covering exclusion. Their rows are N10–N20.
5. **Six citation corrections, all on primary pages** (section d). The most important: Hough–Nielsen is in **Duke Math. J. 168 (2019) 3261–3295**, not Proc. AMS. Klein–Koukoulopoulos–Lemieux is published, in IJNT 20 (2024) 471–479.
6. **One mechanism appears in no source and is derived here (flagged).** Over F_2[T], X² + 1 = (X+1)². So when m is a square, the coset 2ℤ² is algebraically killed, at density 1/4. The `ff/` experiment ignores algebraic factors (grep finds none in `ff/*.py`), so in characteristic 2 its pure-covering numbers understate what a function-field certificate can use. Over ℚ, x² + 1 is irreducible, so this has no ℤ analogue (row N41).
7. **No new YES mechanism and no new NO tool.** Every compositeness mechanism in the slice is on the list in (c). For #203, each one is either (i) a pure coset covering (Y1/Y3/Y5 apply), (ii) a Capelli factorisation (MIXED Theorem E applies), (iii) excluded outright by a one-line argument given in (c), or (iv) the "infinite covering / unbounded least prime factor" kind, for which no instance is known in any setting except purely algebraic ones.

Task items I could not identify: a joint "Jones–Lee" paper; a "Filaseta–Harrington–Jones" paper (the Crossref hit is Finch–Harrington–Jones, row N22); and the "Le" item, which is very likely Mao-Hua Le (Sun–Le, row N23). Searches are listed at the end.

## (c) Every compositeness or covering mechanism in the slice, one line each, with its #203 status

Composite-producing mechanisms (YES side):
- **M1 Pure prime covering of the exponent set.** Each prime kills one residue class mod ord_p(b), or in 2-D one coset of Λ_p. Sources: Erdős 1950 (N1), Sierpiński 1960 (row 9/B1), Selfridge (B1), Riesel (N2), every construction row (12, L22, N22, N25). Status: excluded for e_p ≤ 8·10⁵ (Y1, Y3); any covering needs ≥ 14082 primes with e_p > 8·10⁵ (Y5).
- **M2 Primitive-divisor lift.** Bang/Zsigmondy give, for every modulus n, a prime with that exact order, so any 1-D covering pattern is realised (N1, N19, Caldwell–Komatsu Thm 10 in N21). Status: fails in 2-D. A prescribed lattice need not occur as a Λ_p, and Granville–Pappalardi (row 18) realise patterns only by choosing the bases (L22(4)).
- **M3 CRT on the free multiplier**, used to place each class. Status: transfers to #203 (cosets are arbitrary through m; MIXED augmentation lemma). It is not an obstruction.
- **M4 Trivial factor**: one prime divides every term (CRUS exclusion 1, N27). Status: impossible, since it needs e_p = 1, hence p \| 1.
- **M5 Full algebraic factorisation**: x^q + 1 for odd q, and Germain 4y⁴ + 1 (Izotov row 10; Caldwell–Komatsu Thm 5, N21; CRUS, N27). Status: MIXED §1, Capelli. It covers only the cosets qℤ² and C₄; it does not cover ℤ² alone.
- **M6 Partial covering + algebraic factorisation** (Izotov; FFK; Caldwell–Komatsu base-240 example; CRUS partial-algebraic column; Finch–Jones perfect-power Riesel, N22). Status: excluded for e_p ≤ 8·10⁵ by MIXED Theorem E (Y2).
- **M7 Difference of squares / powers** (Riesel side, CRUS Riesel tables, Finch–Jones). Status: not available, because #203 has the sign +1.
- **M8 Generalised-Fermat class q^m·b^n + 1** (CRUS exclusion 3). This is not a compositeness mechanism but a class without known primes and without a covering. Status: the #203 analogue is m = 1, and 7 is prime.
- **M9 Prime-power lifting** to force ≥ 2 distinct prime factors (Chen 2003, B8; Virbalas, N19; Baczkowski–Eitner, N25; Cohen–Selfridge and Sun, N23). Status: not needed, because compositeness suffices once m > max p.
- **M10 Multiple (t-fold) coverings and coverings with disjoint moduli families** (Harrington, N20; Sun 1999, N32; Filaseta–Kalogirou–Klein Corollary, N12). Status: a special case of M1, excluded a fortiori.
- **M11 Several families with one multiplier**: Brier ±1 (row B7, N25, Filaseta–Juillerat–Luckner), mixed Sierpiński k·2^n + 1 and k + 2^n (N24), many bases (Filaseta–Groth–Luckner), powers k^j (FFK, Caldwell–Komatsu), consecutive k (Finch-Smith–Groth). Status: fails for #203. The rows of #203 are not independent families, because each prime acts on all rows through one lattice (verdict/4 C4; L22(1)).
- **M12 Two exponents through the exponent difference**, with a Fermat chain on v₂(a − b) (Crocker; Sun–Le, N23). Status: no analogue. The killed sets are cosets, and a valuation chain is infinite (kind iii) or else a finite pure covering (M1).
- **M13 Homogeneous (sublattice) coverings of ℤ²** (Cremona–Koymans, row 19). Status: impossible for #203 (Y7, the Fermat argument).
- **M14 Coset coverings of ℤ² by cyclic-quotient lattices** (Granville–Pappalardi, row 18). This is the #203 certificate type, so it is M1 in 2-D.
- **M15 Infinite covering / unbounded least prime factor**: Erdős's covering conjecture as in FFK and Caldwell–Komatsu (N21 Thm 5 and Open Question); strong infinite coverings (Harrington–Klein–Lowrance–Trifonov, N13); erdosproblems #1113 (row 4). Status: verdict/4 N4 "kind (iii)". No instance is known in any setting except purely algebraic ones (M5). This is owner 8's residual case.
- **M16 Ring and polynomial variants** (Jones Lucas and polynomial; Filaseta–Harrington; Finch–Harrington–Jones nonlinear; Jones–White O_d, N22; Schinzel reducibility, N31; function-field coverings, N16–N18). Status: one exponent with a free multiplier, so not usable. The algebraic part reduces to Capelli for binomials.
- **M17 Pullback of a 1-D covering along a common primitive vector** (L22, RESULTS D2). Status: a subfamily of M1, excluded with it.
- **M18 Characteristic-p inseparable factor** X^p + 1 = (X + 1)^p, including p = 2 (N41, derived). Status: function-field analogue only. No ℤ analogue for p = 2; for odd p it is the Capelli case already in M5.

Non-existence mechanisms for coverings (the tools that exclude YES certificates):
- **E1 Haight / first-order Bonferroni**: δ ≥ α − β (FFKPY Lemma 2.1, N9; L2). Status: weaker than the repo's distortion bound.
- **E2 Third-order Bonferroni with coprime pairs and triples** (Harrington–Klein–Lowrance–Trifonov Thm 1.9, N13). Status: a 2-D version is unproved; owner 1.
- **E3 Sieving by large integers**: average over residues mod the Q-smooth lcm, with a pair sum ≪ s² log²(QK)/Q (FFKPY, N9). Status: the 1-D prototype of the tail; owner 4.

Status: this is the engine of Y1/Y3/Y5.

- **E6 Hyperplane covers of product boxes** (BBMST ANT, N5; exposition Thm 2.1, L6). Status: the #203 torus is a subgroup, not a box; owner 1.
- **E7 Size lower bounds**: Simpson/Schleicher 1 + Σα_j(q_j − 1) (row 11, N21); Cremona–Koymans Thm B (row 19); Lettl–Sun coset bound (row 20, Y6). Status: Y6 is superseded by Y5.
